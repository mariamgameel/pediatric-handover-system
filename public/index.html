Clinical Architecture

Engineered Specifically for Inpatient Pediatrics

Traditional hospital EMRs are burdened by complex billing, appointments, and fragmented screens. This system delivers high-density clinical clarity designed around bedside shift change workflows.

Shift Handover No Retyping

Automated Handover Synthesis

Dynamically compiles up-to-the-minute active problems, ongoing IV fluids, oxygen delivery modes (HFNC, CPAP), pending investigations, and unhandled tasks into a structured handover card.

Clinical Rule: Automatically flags "Critical — Verbal Handover Required" when deterioration or abnormal lab values are active.
Rapid Deterioration Emergency Flow

1-Click Bedside Escalation

Immediately records clinical triggers, actions taken, and escalation tiers (PICU review, Emergency Code). Escalates patient status to Critical with fixed clinical reason validation and ward alerts.

Safety Guard: Enforces mandatory clinical reasons (respiratory distress, convulsions, desaturation, poor perfusion) from a vetted list.
Clinical Safety Immutable History

Zero Overwrite Architecture

Physiological vitals are append-only. Management plans use an immutable versioned stack (v1, v2, ...) authored by Specialists and Consultants; old plans are superseded, never destroyed.

Audit Integrity: Every status update, resolution note, and prescription is permanently bound to actor Staff ID and timestamp.
Access Control Shift-Gated

Shift-Based Lockout & Grace Buffer

Restricts medical record access strictly to scheduled clinical shifts with a 45-minute handover buffer before and after shift. Prevents off-shift access while supporting instant emergency overrides.

Override Mechanism: System Administrator Amira Fouad can issue temporary 4h to 24h emergency overrides during crisis coverage.
Intelligence Delta Engine

'What Changed?' Delta Calculation

When a doctor re-opens a patient, the system surfaces meaningful deltas since their last visit: status escalations, unreviewed lab results, completed tasks, and consultant updates.

Lightweight Model: Powered by PatientView tracking, ensuring doctors never miss overnight clinical developments.
Diagnostics 4-Stage Lifecycle

Result Review Governance

Strict distinction between Requested, Pending, Result Available, and Reviewed. Highlights unreviewed results on the dashboard and handover sheet until signed off by senior staff.

Abnormal Lab Alert: Critical abnormal results automatically trigger high-priority alerts across the clinical team.
